EM_MK5 diagrams and schematics

October 2026. Matches EM_MK5_project_summary_v2.md. Red dashed lines are fault and enable paths. Items marked [CONFIRM] in the summary are still to be verified.

Sheet 1: control, ADC and sensors

Pi 5, guarded position sensors, SPI0 bus to the RP2040 and AD4691, supply dividers, ULN2803A LED drivers, trip latch and enable optocouplers.

Sheet 2: power path and protection, one channel

Bus feed through the CT, coil, paralleled SiC switches, shunt and battery, with the trip chain and the gate drive blocks. Channel 2 is a copy.

Sheet 3: gate drive groups

Coil control group (four Q1 gates) and regeneration group (four Q2 gates), each with four PWM optocouplers, two 2EDN7534R drivers and one enable optocoupler. The red note marks the known reference problem in the regeneration group.

Schematic: power stage, one channel

Coil, flyback diode and snubbers, paralleled Q1 and Q2 pairs, shunts, battery and the coil current sense. Gate nets G1a, G1b, G2a, G2b go to the drivers.

Schematic: gate driver for one MOSFET pair

Two ACPL-P343 PWM optocouplers into the two inputs of a 2EDN7534R, a third ACPL-P343 for the shared enable, and separate 10 Ω gate resistors to the two paralleled MOSFETs. Repeated four times.

Schematic: LED drivers for the ACPL-343 (ULN2803A)

One ULN2803A sinks the LED current of up to 8 PWM optocouplers from the +5 V rail, and a second ULN2803A does the same for the enable optocouplers. A high input turns the LED on, so the PWM and enable polarity are unchanged.

Schematic: four trip latches and enable

Each trip comparator resets its own latch in one 74HC279, so the Pi can see which trip fired. The four Q outputs are combined by a BAT54 wired-AND into the enable node, which drives a ULN2803A input and two ACPL-343 enable optocouplers (LED current from +5 V).

Schematic: current trip front end

CT on the +60 V feed, 56 Ω burden, TVS, BAT54 peak hold, and the LTC6752 (SOT-23-5, 3.3 V) comparator with a 1.88 V reference and hysteresis. TRIP_N goes to the latch /R input.

Schematic: measurement inputs

INA240 outputs and NTC dividers into the AD4691 with a 4.096 V reference, and the supply voltage divider into an RP2040 ADC input.

Schematic: position sensor input guard

One channel, repeated for S1, S2 and S3. A PNP sensor output drives an optocoupler LED, and the transistor side has a pull-up, series resistor and BAT54S clamp before the Pi GPIO.

Schematic: INA240 backup trip

INA240 outputs through the same LTC6752 comparators, each driving its own latch /R input. Catches a stuck-on switch and slower overcurrents. One coil channel is shown in detail.
