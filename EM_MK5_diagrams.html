EM_MK5 diagrams and schematics

October 2026. Matches EM_MK5_project_summary_v2.md. Red dashed lines are fault and enable paths. Items marked [CONFIRM] in the summary are still to be verified.

Sheet 1: control, ADC and sensors

Pi 5, guarded position sensors, SPI0 bus to the RP2040 and AD4691, supply dividers, ULN2803A (PWM) LED drivers and a 74HCT21 AND (enable), current and temperature trip latches and enable optocouplers.

Sheet 2: power path and protection, one channel

Bus feed through the CT, coil, paralleled SiC switches, shunt and battery, with the trip chain and the gate drive blocks. Channel 2 is a copy.

Sheet 3: gate drive groups

Coil control group (four Q1 gates) and regeneration group (four Q2 gates), each with four PWM optocouplers, two 2EDN7534R drivers and one enable optocoupler. The red note marks the known reference problem in the regeneration group.

Schematic: power stage, one channel

Coil, flyback diode and snubbers, paralleled Q1 and Q2 pairs, shunts, battery and the coil current sense. Gate nets G1a, G1b, G2a, G2b go to the drivers.

Schematic: gate driver for one MOSFET pair

Two ACPL-P343 PWM optocouplers into the two inputs of a 2EDN7534R, a third ACPL-P343 for the shared enable, and separate 10 Ω gate resistors to the two paralleled MOSFETs. Repeated four times.

Schematic: LED drivers for the ACPL-343 (ULN2803A and direct drive)

One ULN2803A sinks the LED current of the 8 PWM optocouplers from the +5 V rail. The enable optocouplers need no transistor: the enable node (output of a 74HCT21 AND on +5 V) drives each LED directly through its own 470 Ω to GND (about 5 mA). A high input turns the LED on in both cases, so the polarity is unchanged.

Schematic: two trip latches and enable

The merged current trip (TRIP_I) and the temperature trip (TRIP_T) each reset one latch in a 74LS279 (5 V TTL). Both Q outputs go to Pi GPIOs through dividers and into a 74HCT21 4-input AND (5 V), whose output is the enable node. It drives the ACPL-343 enable LEDs directly, each through 470 Ω, with no transistor.

Schematic: current trip front end

CT on the +60 V feed, 56 Ω burden, TVS, BAT54 peak hold, and the LTC6752 (SOT-23-5, 3.3 V) comparator with a 1.88 V reference and hysteresis. TRIP_N joins the other current signals in the merged current trip, which uses one shared LTC6752 (this drawing shows its comparator).

Schematic: measurement inputs

INA240 outputs and NTC dividers into the AD4691 with a 4.096 V reference, and the supply voltage divider into an RP2040 ADC input.

Schematic: position sensor input guard

One channel, repeated for S1, S2 and S3. A PNP sensor output drives an optocoupler LED, and the transistor side has a pull-up, series resistor and BAT54S clamp before the Pi GPIO.

Schematic: merged current trip (one comparator)

The CT peak-hold signal and the three INA240 signals (coil 1, coil 2, battery) are scaled to the same level and joined by a BAT54 diode-OR, so the highest one reaches a single LTC6752. Its output TRIP_I resets the current latch.

Schematic: radiator temperature trip (one comparator)

The five NTC divider nodes are joined by a BAT54 diode-OR for the lowest voltage (the hottest radiator). One LTC6752 (the same part as the current trip) watches it. Output TRIP_T resets the temperature latch.
